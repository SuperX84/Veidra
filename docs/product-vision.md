# Veidra product vision

Veidra should be the operational profitability layer for small and medium construction companies in Norway.

## Core promise

Help construction companies avoid lost revenue by connecting deviations, hours, extra work, contracts and invoice preparation.

## First MVP

The first sellable MVP should focus on:

- importing projects and hours from Kvalitetskontroll;
- identifying deviations that can become extra work;
- creating or reusing hour types tied to Tripletex `Ekstra arbeid`;
- preparing invoice-ready lines for Tripletex;
- showing project margin risk early.

## Positioning

Veidra is not another HMS app and not a replacement for Tripletex.

Veidra sits between SmartKalk, Kvalitetskontroll and Tripletex:

```text
SmartKalk / Kvalitetskontroll / Tripletex
        -> Veidra
        -> prosjektstatus, avvik, ekstra arbeid, fakturagrunnlag
```
